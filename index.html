What is DPC

More than a booking. A collective.

Delhi Padel Collective brings the city's padel players together: first-timers and ranked players, on the same courts, every week. We run the games, keep the score, and turn every match into a rating you can watch move. You bring the racket.

Questions

What people ask us.

I'm new to padel. Can I join?

Yes. Beginner+ americanos are built for it, and most people rally in their first hour. Padel is always doubles, on a 10 × 20 m glass-walled court; the walls are in play, rackets are solid, and scoring is like tennis.

What does a game cost?

Americanos are ₹1,400–1,500 per player for about 90 minutes, court included. Tournament nights cost a little more.

Where do you play?

South Delhi courts, most evenings: South Ex and RK Puram. Each game shows its venue when you book.

What are Pts and rating?

Pts are the points you earn from every match: where you finish in an Americano and the games you win in a tournament. They set your rank. Your rating is your level on the 0–7 scale. Read the full explainer
