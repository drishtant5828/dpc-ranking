Privacy policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

Delhi Padel Collective ("DPC", "we", "us") is operated by Rebound Rally Private Limited, 79, Sunder Nagar, Lodhi Road, New Delhi 110003. GSTIN 07AANCR6880R1Z7.

What we collect

  • Details you give us when you sign up, register or contact us: name, phone number, email, gender, date of birth, Instagram handle, and your padel experience, level and availability.
  • Your game history, ratings and rankings when you play with us.
  • Payment details are handled by Razorpay. We receive confirmation that you paid, but never your card or bank details.
  • Anonymous website usage (pages visited and how forms are used), measured with our own tools. We don't use advertising cookies.

How we use it

  • To confirm and run your bookings, place you in games and teams, and keep ratings and leaderboards.
  • To contact you on WhatsApp, phone or email about your bookings and our events.
  • For the Delhi Padel League, your playing profile (not your contact details) is shared with franchise owners for team selection.
  • To improve our website and events.

Who we share it with

Only with service providers who help us run DPC (such as Razorpay for payments, and Google and Supabase for storing records), venues where needed for your booking, and where the law requires. We don't sell your data.

How long we keep it

For as long as you play with us, and as long as needed for our records and legal obligations.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent. Contact us and we'll respond within 30 days.
