Service delivery policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

Delhi Padel Collective ("DPC", "we", "us") is operated by Rebound Rally Private Limited, 79, Sunder Nagar, Lodhi Road, New Delhi 110003. GSTIN 07AANCR6880R1Z7.

No shipping

We provide services, not physical products, so nothing is shipped. Any kit included with a booking (such as Delhi Padel League T-shirts) is handed over in person at the venue.

How services are delivered

  • Games, coaching, events and League matches take place in person at padel venues in Delhi NCR, on the date and time of your booking.
  • After you pay, we confirm your booking on WhatsApp, usually within 24 hours.
  • We share the venue, timing and any other details before the session or event.
  • Sponsorship and partnership deliverables are provided as set out in the signed agreement.

If something changes

If we change or cancel a session, we let you know on WhatsApp as early as possible. See our refund and cancellation policy.
