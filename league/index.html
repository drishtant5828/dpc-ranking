Questions

What people ask us.

The league

What is DPL?

Delhi NCR's first franchise-based padel league. Eight franchises and 120 players compete across three weekends for one title.

When and where is Season One?

The group stage is 20–22 November and the semis & finals are 28–29 November, in New Delhi.

Who runs it?

The team behind Delhi Padel Collective, a community of 1,000+ members that has run 45+ events since February 2026.

Players

How do I get a spot?

Register to play. We confirm your entry and category on WhatsApp, and franchises build their squads at auction night.

What level do I need to be?

Every level can play. You pick up to two categories when you register; the DPC team confirms your final category based on level and entries.

Franchise owners

How many franchises are there?

Eight. Send an enquiry and we'll walk you through ownership on a call.

Partners

How many partners will there be?

Nine: one Title, three Associate and five Category. Associate and Category partners get exclusivity in their industry.

What happens after I enquire?

A fifteen-minute call, then a proposal built around your brand within two working days.
