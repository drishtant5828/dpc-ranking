What is DPC

More than a booking. A collective.

Delhi Padel Collective brings the city's padel players together: first-timers and ranked players, on the same courts, every week.

We run the games, keep the score, and turn every match into a rating you can watch move. You bring the racket.

Questions

What people ask us.

I'm new to padel. Can I join?
Yes. Beginner+ americanos are built for it, and most people rally in their first hour.
  • Always doubles: four players on a 10 × 20 m glass-walled court.
  • The walls are in play. Let the ball come off the glass.
  • Solid rackets, no strings. Underarm serve, tennis scoring.
What does a game cost?
Americanos are ₹1,400–1,500 per player for about 90 minutes, court included. Tournament nights cost a little more.
Where do you play?
South Delhi courts, most evenings: South Ex and RK Puram. Each game shows its venue when you book.
How does the rating work?
Your rating runs from 0 to 7. It blends your self-rating with how you finish in americanos and tournaments, and you're ranked after two matches. Read the full explainer
